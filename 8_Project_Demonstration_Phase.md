### 8. Project Demonstration Phase

Objective

To demonstrate the completed ComicCraft AI Comic Story Creator and explain its features, workflow, and working process.

Activities

• Presented the completed ComicCraft application.
• Demonstrated how users can enter a story prompt.
• Showed how Google Gemini generates the comic story.
• Demonstrated character and dialogue generation.
• Explained the generation of comic scenes.
• Showed the main features and user interface of the application.
• Explained the overall workflow and project implementation.

Result

The completed ComicCraft project was successfully demonstrated, and its main features, working process, and use of Google Gemini models were clearly presented.
