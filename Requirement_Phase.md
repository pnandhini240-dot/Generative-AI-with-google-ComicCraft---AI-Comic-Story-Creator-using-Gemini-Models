### Requirement Phase

Objective

To identify the basic requirements needed to develop the ComicCraft AI Comic Story Creator using Google Gemini models.

Activities

• Identified the required software and hardware.
• Identified the tools and technologies needed for development.
• Identified the requirements for AI-based story generation.
• Planned user input and AI-generated output features.
• Identified the requirements for generating characters, dialogues, and comic scenes.
• Planned a simple and user-friendly interface.

Result

The basic requirements for developing the ComicCraft project were successfully identified and organized.
