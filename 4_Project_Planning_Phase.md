### 4. Project Planning Phase

Objective

To plan the development process, tasks, resources, and time required to complete the ComicCraft AI Comic Story Creator successfully.

Activities

• Divided the project into different development stages.
• Planned the tasks required for each stage of the project.
• Allocated time for development, testing, and documentation.
• Planned the integration of Google Gemini models into the application.
• Identified the tools and resources required for development.
• Planned regular testing and improvements during development.
• Prepared a clear workflow to complete the project within the planned schedule.

Result

A proper project development plan was prepared with clearly defined tasks, resources, and stages for completing the ComicCraft project successfully.
